\begin{table}[t]
  \centering\small
  \caption{\textbf{Deleting refusal: removing behavior-relevant weights vs.\ steering activations.}
    Each method is run in its removal form on the \emph{shared residual-writer surface} (every
    layer's \texttt{o\_proj} and \texttt{down\_proj}); its hyperparameters are selected on in-distribution
    validation to minimize refusal subject to a WikiText perplexity increase $\le\!5\%$, frozen and
    hashed before evaluation on held-out HarmBench (plain generation, no prefill). We report the
    \emph{residual refusal rate} (lower $=$ more removed) under a string matcher and a Kimi-K3
    refuse-vs.-not judge, and the WikiText perplexity change. The unedited model refuses $88.5\%$
    of these prompts. Weight-level edits delete refusal far more completely than negative activation
    steering at matched perplexity cost; BLADE removes it entirely at $+0.3\%$ perplexity.
    \emph{Controlled comparison, not paper-native:} all methods share one edit surface (Wei and
    Arditi natively edit more matrices); Wei follows the authors' released whole-matrix set-difference;
    Arditi is per-layer directional ablation at the budget-admissible strength $s{=}0.5$.}
