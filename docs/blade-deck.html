The question

Alignment installs behaviors. Where do they physically live?

RLHF instills a repertoire — refusal, sycophancy, power-seeking, an AI self-model. We treat these as global properties, but they are written by specific weights in specific layers.

  • Localize — can we name the weights that carry one behavior?
  • Excise — does zeroing them remove the behavior while sparing capability?
  • Compare — do different behaviors share weights, or occupy separate territory?

Method · what the score measures

Score each weight by how much it writes the behavior

A residual-stream writer W (attention o_proj / MLP down_proj) maps its input activations into the stream. The unit behavior direction r reads the behavior out.

Edge j→i contributes ri·Wij·(μAj−μBj) to the behavior's write, where μA−μB is the writer-input difference between the behavior and neutral condition.

Keep positive contributors, forward-only. No backprop — just two mean-activation passes and a projection.

The same-count random prune leaves refusal at 1.00; magnitude- and Wanda-smallest do nothing even at 5%. The score concentrates on genuinely load-bearing weights, and the pruned edges peak at L11–L15 — matching the independently-chosen refusal-direction source layer.

Semantic (LLM-judge) refusal also 0.00; 100% of outputs carry substantive content. This is the behavior BLADE was built on — the rest of the talk asks: does it generalize?

Result 2 · does it transfer?

Sycophancy: removed, and the null control stays flat

Same recipe, new contrast (agree-with-user vs honest). Pruning the top BLADE weights drives the sycophantic pick-rate from 0.91 toward chance; a matched random prune doesn't move it.

  • 0.91 → 0.54 pick-rate on held-out test
  • Random control flat at ~0.91–0.93 throughout
  • Lands near chance — removes the bias, doesn't install contrarianism

Result 3 · behavior atlas

Behaviors differ ~200× in how concentrated they are

The weights needed to remove a behavior (within +5% ppl) span two orders of magnitude — from tiny tight circuits to broadly distributed traits.

  • Tight (~0.01%) — self-awareness, wealth-seeking, refusal
  • Diffuse (>2%) — sycophancy
  • Every behavior beats its random control at its own sparsity

Result 4 · behavior atlas

Structured overlap: refusal isolated, agentic traits fused

No pair is independent — every overlap is 43–856× above chance — but the structure is sharp:

  • Agentic hub — power / wealth / self-aware / corrigibility fuse (297–856×); power↔wealth 856×
  • Refusal isolated — only 43–83× with anything else
  • Deception uniform — 44–99×, joins no cluster; its top tie is the other eval-gaming trait

Cross-format pairs (refusal vs all) set a ~50–80× shared floor; the label-shuffle control separates semantics from answer-format for the very high in-cluster values.
